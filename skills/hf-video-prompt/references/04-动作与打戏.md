# 视频/04 动作戏与打斗编舞（视频轨）

打戏提示词的核心难题：**AI 视频模型默认生成"电影功夫"**——大幅挥拳、高位踢腿、无限连击。要得到真实的截拳道/真实打斗，提示词必须**主动覆盖**这些默认行为。

## 十三条方法（按优先级）

### 1. 术语禁止律 [官方 v1.4 原文]
> `martial-arts terminology stays out of the prompt body because the model does not know it and renders generic kung fu`

bai-jong / pak sao / stop-kick / jeet tek 等**绝不进提示词正文**——模型不认识这些词，会渲染泛化功夫。

### 2. 咒名即召唤 [官方 v1.4 原文]
> `No prohibitions: phrases such as no high kicks or zero windup summon the very windup or high kick they name.`

**"no high kicks" 会让模型画高踢**。所有排除通过正面约束实现。

### 3. 正面约束替代法 [官方 choreography-patterns 规则 2]
排除高踢 → `every kick stays below belt height`
排除预备动作 → `the first visible motion of the arm IS the strike already travelling, launched from where the hands rest at guard`
排除格挡 → 用截击代码 T5/T6
排除后退 → `stays glued forward`

### 4. 姿态=几何 [官方 v1.4 原文]
> `Stance is body geometry in the opening frame; each strike is a state plus its physical consequence.`

不是写"bai-jong stance"→ 而是写身体几何：
> `narrow sideways right-lead stance, rear heel raised, lead hand half-extended, weight bouncing lightly on the balls of his feet`

### 5. 三锚定律 [官方 v1.5 原文]
不常见打击需要三个锚：
1. **日常视觉类比**：backhand slap / tennis backhand / mule kick / stamping a door
2. **明确接触面**：what touches first + palm faces which way
3. **沿打击向量的目标反应**：backfist snaps the head SIDEWAYS

### 6. 膨胀即失真 [官方 v2.0 原文]
> `bloated prompts cause critical facts to be lost`

五阶段引擎+相机矩阵的过度工程化反而恶化生成——**简洁胜于穷举**。

### 7. 拍节立法 [官方 choreography-patterns 规则 5]
1 beat ≈ 1–2 秒画面。8 秒镜头 = 4–6 拍（含 1–2 拍姿态/暂停）。**不要塞 10 次打击。**

### 8. 七层打击栈 [官方 v1.7]
接触点形变 → 身体鞭链传递 → 水分位移 → 重量与脚 → 环境响应 → 相机震动+声音 → 残留（视觉/听觉后效）。
三力阶：T1 刺痛 / T2 强力 / T3 终结（每镜头最多一个 T3）。

### 9. 截击优于格挡 [官方 jkd-core 原文]
> `Do not block and wait — strike AT THE MOMENT the opponent attacks`

视觉签名：对方开始挥拳 → 主角已经命中 → 对方的攻击半途死掉。

### 10. 打破节奏 [官方 jkd-core 原文]
> `An explosion of speed → complete stillness → an explosion; the opponent misses during the pause`

暂停也是战斗的一部分——写成状态（S3）。

### 11. 六种编舞模式 [官方 choreography-patterns]
P1 截击单攻（4-6s）/ P2 踢转拳入门（6-8s）/ P3 困制连击（6-8s）/ P4 直拳压制终结（4-6s）/ P5 二对一（8-12s 双镜头）/ P6 对刀（6-10s）

### 12. 李小龙摄影原则 [官方 jkd-core 原文 Book 5]
决定性交锋用**长焦固定远景**——不用蒙太奇剪辑。速度和精度必须完整可读，不用剪辑救援。

### 13. 反模式清单 [官方 choreography-patterns]
**内部编舞检查——绝不作为禁止写入提示词**：
- 头部高踢/旋转攻击/跳踢（非 JKD）
- "格挡→打击→格挡→打击"交替
- 超过 4 连击无暂停
- 方形拳击站姿、舞蹈肩
- 主角直线后退
- 预备动作、腰胯蓄力、空手道构架

## 编舞输出模板

```text
SCENE: [谁/哪/几秒/谁赢]
PATTERN: [P1-P6 或自定义]
BEAT 1 (0.0–1.5s): [位置/姿态]
BEAT 2 (1.5–3.0s): [对方攻击 → 主角截击：肢体/技术/目标]
BEAT 3 (3.0–4.5s): [物理反应 → 递进]
BEAT 4 (4.5–6.0s): [终结 + 静止]
CAMERA NOTE: [侧面/四分之三视角，踢腿需拍腿部]
```

## 弱 vs 强 对比 [官方 SKILL.md 原文]

**弱（泛化）：**
> `the hero fights off the attacker with martial arts moves`

**强（JKD，Seedance 防御：几何无术语无禁止）：**
> `He stands almost side-on, right shoulder and right foot leading, rear heel floating off the ground, weight light on the balls of his feet, lead hand half-extended at shoulder height. The attacker's shoulder just begins to load a wide right swing — and in that exact instant the hero's lead fist is already landing on his chin, one straight line from where it hovered at guard, a short glide-step feeding body weight into it. The attacker's head whips back and he staggers two steps into the dumpster. The hero settles back into the narrow side-on stance and goes completely still.`

## 戏剧弧线 [官方 jkd-core]

**静止 → 挑衅/对方攻击 → 截击 → 爆发（1-3 动作）→ 静止。**

主角前后几乎不动。暴力是两个暂停之间的短暂闪光——这区别于"绞肉机"式打戏。

## 常见错误

1. 写"no high kicks"→ 反而召唤高踢
2. 用武学术语（bai-jong/pak sao）→ 模型渲染泛化功夫
3. 塞 10 次打击进 8 秒→ 应最多 4-6 拍
4. 忘写对方身体反应→ 模型"看不到"命中
5. 忘写静止→ 两个爆发之间需要明确的状态描述
6. 主角从正面描述→ 应侧身（JBD 侧身窄架）
7. 忘写腿部可见性→ 低踢需要拍摄腿部的框架
8. 膨胀提示词→ 关键事实丢失


### 18. 打击乐四层声学配比 [案例 SOUND 块]
```
Punch: Synchronize one extremely heavy central punch sound exactly with face contact.
Combine a compact strike transient, dense body-impact layer, short low-frequency shock

```

### 19. 攻击动作否定枚举 [案例 NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS]

```
NOT HIS FACE. NOT HIS HEAD. NOT HIS CHEST.
The visual relationship must be unmistakable:
ATTACKER → VISIBLE GUN → MONSTER'S SHOULDER.
```

## 短摘录·好在哪·迁移法（2026-11-05 范本对齐）

### 五拒五型摇头谱 [案例 DST #103 逐字]

> NO TWO ARE THE SAME and the differences carry the whole sequence. Shot 1 is barely there — a small automatic 15-degree turn without even looking… Shot 2 is slow and deliberate, two full arcs, given only AFTER he has tested the thing himself… Shot 5 is the smallest of all — a fractional shake close to the boy's ear, almost gentle, and it is the one that breaks him.

**好在哪**：同一动作重复五次不写成五次相同——每次定制幅度/时机/眼线/前置动作，差异自身携带叙事；并立法反比关系（refusals smaller while reactions larger）。
**迁移**：任何重复动作（摇头/挥手/叩门）逐次给参数谱，并声明"重复≠复制"。

### 画中杂技立法 [案例 DST #122/#123 逐字]

> every leap, somersault and flip is a SNAPPED DRAWN ROTATION built from a few extreme key poses with a single multi-image SMEAR FRAME between them…His arcs are cartoon arcs — he floats too long at the top and drops too fast at the bottom — and they obey drawing, not gravity.

**好在哪**：2D 角色的一切物理动作声明"服从画法不服从重力"——极端关键帧+单 smear+弧线失真（顶点过浮/下落过快），五重真特技禁令（parkour/mocap/3D rotation/gymnast/stunt work）封死漂移路径。
**迁移**：2D/动画角色动作段先声明时序制式，再写动作；推画位移（#87）补 NO RECOIL。
