## 1. 角色板引用制度

### UUID 变量引用 [案例 VENGEANCE/SBS/7L 全量]

> <<<7bdaae3f-c213-448a-8a06-276e3bf611aa>>> Kenji — 75yo man, silver hair, grey plazer, navy bowtie.
**好在哪**：每个角色有一块定妆板（UUID）；prompt 中用 <<<uuid>>> 引用=告诉模型「按这张图生成这个人」；同一 UUID 跨条出现=角色一致性锚。
**迁移**：角色定妆图上传后得 UUID，prompt 中用 <<<uuid>>> + 一行外观摘要引用。

### @命名短引用 [案例 7L #123/#185]

> @Alma is an ordinary human-sized woman (~1.7m) ／ @Hope, once she appears, stays insect-sized
**好在哪**：@命名替代 UUID——更短更可读，同一板可用多个 @别名（@Alma / @Hope / @scale_alma_hope）。
**迁移**：法头或 ACTIVE REFERENCES 块定义 @Name → UUID 映射；后续用 @Name 引用。

### ACTIVE REFERENCES 逐板声明 [案例 7L #115 逐字]

> <<<Alma>>>, voice_id: Vera. Exactly one instance of her in every frame — no duplicate. 100% matches reference. ／ <<<Hope>>>, voice_id: Luna…100% matches reference.
**好在哪**：每块参考板独立一行声明：角色名+voice_id+出现频率+100% matches reference 硬承诺。
**迁移**：法头或 ACTIVE REFERENCES 块逐板写：板名/voice_id/每帧出现次数/100% matches reference。

### 板型体系：外观板 vs 状态板 [案例 7L 全量]

> 外观板：<<<67c711ab>>>（战斗龙）/ <<<c6f61b9a>>>（盘踞龙）/ <<<79912551>>>（站立龙）
> 状态板：<<<58ab68f8>>>（冰困状态）/ <<<7aa087af>>>（Hope 翅膀失色）/ <<<ed364edb>>>（Hope 医院版）
**好在哪**：同一角色可以有多块板——外观板（不同姿态/场景）+ 状态板（受伤/冰封/失色）——各板独立 UUID 各归各场景。
**迁移**：角色有多状态时建板族：基础板+状态板+场景板；prompt 只引当前状态那块板。

## 2. 场景/位置板引用

### 场景板=Geometry lock [案例 7L 法头全量]

> Geometry lock: <<<3f317391>>> is a fixed closed set with its own distinct stone material. No doors, doorways, portals, openings, windows, arches, passages…anywhere in the location.
**好在哪**：场景板不只给视觉——还锁建筑规则（无门无窗无洞），防模型自由发挥加建筑元素。
**迁移**：场景板引用后加建筑规则条款（no X, no Y, no Z）；fixed closed set 声明防场景漂移。

### 跨场景门材质归属 [案例 7L #81]

> doorway rendered in <<<c97156a8>>>'s own stone material, identical size to the <<<31395eb4>>> side.
**好在哪**：两个场景之间的门，门框材质分两侧各归各墙——穿越门时材质过渡写清楚。
**迁移**：跨场景元素（门/桥/通道）写「门框材质各归各场景+尺寸一致」。

### 依图重建令 [案例 SBS #285 + 7L #237]

> based on image_0.png…All elements from image_0.png, including the specific objects on the shelves, the scroll design, and the rock garden layout, must be preserved.
**好在哪**：从生成图迭代下一版时写 based on image_N.png + All elements must be preserved 三级清单（家具级/纹样级/布局级）。
**迁移**：依图迭代写 based on image_N.png + 三级保全清单；引用编号从 0 起。

## 3. 板的权限分级

### 参考图权限分级 [案例 DST/SBS/7L 全量]

> #SBS：参考图只给外观不给构图——仅按图片原貌，无眼镜（维持图片原貌，无眼镜）
> #7L：This reference is used for scale only — nothing else. It has no influence on framing, camera angle, camera position, or composition; it has no influence on color, lighting, or grading.
> #DST：FOCUS LOCK per the blocking reference
**好在哪**：参考图不是"照抄"而是"取某一维度"——外观板取面部/服装、比例板取身高比、灰模取机位——每个参考图声明"取什么不取什么"。
**迁移**：每张参考图在 prompt 中写权限声明：used for [维度] only — no influence on [不取的维度]。

### BLOCKING REFERENCE 灰模机位权威 [案例 7L #84 首见]

> An attached grey 3D blockout video defines this shot exactly. Follow it for camera position, camera movement, lens, framing, timing, and the position and movement of every character. The blockout's grey untextured look defines none of the final image — all surfaces, materials, lighting and colour come from the locked references.
**好在哪**：灰模视频=机位权威、外观归锁定参考的双轨分工——一句 "grey look defines none of the final image" 防灰模外观渗入成片。
**迁移**：有 previz/blockout 时写 BLOCKING REFERENCE 块+权限二分（运动归灰模、观感归参考）。

### 隐形计算板 [案例 7L #57/#107]

> <<<5b1f4e83>>> is the internal size-comparison reference locking Hope's size relative to Alma...used purely for scale calculation and not itself a visible character or element in frame.
**好在哪**：比例参考板声明 used purely for scale calculation and not itself a visible character or element in frame——模型只取尺寸不画它。
**迁移**：辅助板写 not itself a visible character or element in frame + used purely for [用途]。

## 4. 剪影复制换皮法

### 像素级复制再 reshape [案例 7L #4/#5 逐字]

> duplicate the existing standing human figure already in this photo — copy its exact silhouette, its exact height in pixels, its exact distance from camera, and its exact position on the ground plane — and place this duplicate off to one side…reshape and re-skin it to become <<<c49c418d>>>.
**好在哪**：新增角色不重画——从已有角色的剪影复制（像素级），再换皮（reshape 成新角色外观）；高度永不超过被复制者。
**迁移**：在已有画面加新角色写「copy exact silhouette + reshape to become X + height never exceeds original」。

### 足迹不变条款 [案例 7L #2 逐字]

> A standing pose naturally reads taller and narrower than a coiled one, but its total mass and the ground area it occupies should stay consistent with its already-correct scale here.
**好在哪**：姿态变而占地不变——standing pose 天然更高更窄但总质量和占地必须与盘踞版一致；pose change without scale change。
**迁移**：姿态变换写「pose 变 but footprint/mass stays consistent」防模型放大。

## 5. 跨板取眼移植

### 单特征跨板移植 [案例 7L #103]

> The eye's color, iris pattern, pupil shape, and immediate eyelid texture come from <<<67c711ab>>> exactly, replacing only that one feature; every other part…stays 100% identical.
**好在哪**：从 A 板取单件（眼）装到 B 板——四要素清单（color/iris/pupil/eyelid）+replacing only that one feature。
**迁移**：单特征移植写「X's [feature] come from [另一板] exactly, replacing only that one feature」。

### 眼口双板 [案例 7L #266]

> Using <<<6093eb96>>> as the exact reference for the eye and the open mouth — match its eye color, iris detail, glossy surface, and its open-mouth shape, teeth, and jaw angle precisely.
**好在哪**：同一张参考图可同时供眼和口两个特征——分别声明 match 其 eye color/shape 和 mouth shape/teeth/jaw angle。
**迁移**：一张参考图供多特征时逐特征声明 match 范围。

### 换眼+除反射 [案例 7L #278]

> While placing the eye, remove the reflection of a person visible in the eye's surface from image_1 — the transferred eye should show no human reflection, only a plausible reflection of its new surroundings.
**好在哪**：换眼后旧眼中的人影反射要清理——transferred eye should show no human reflection, only a plausible reflection of its new surroundings。
**迁移**：器官移植后检查旧反射/阴影/倒影是否需清理并写清理条款。

## 6. 同景双板角关系

### 同走廊正反角 [案例 7L #177 vs #128 两版互斥实录]

> #177：4ca94198 and c97156a8 are **two camera angles of the SAME real stone corridor** — c97156a8 is the reverse angle, in which the tally-mark wall is behind camera and therefore **no tally marks appear anywhere in shot**.
> #128：c97156a8 is a **distinct, separate symbolic space**…**the two never appear together**.
**好在哪**：同一对板的关系在不同 prompt 中有两版语义（同景正反角 vs 异空间互斥）——各条内部自洽，跨条语义漂移。
**迁移**：板间关系（同景异角 vs 异空间）逐条显式声明并在台账记关系版本。

### 空间登记制 [案例 7L #128/#169]

> 4ca94198 is the REAL-WORLD location, used only in Cut 1, Cut 2, and (once revealed) Cut 8. c97156a8 (used ONLY for the six memory loops) is a distinct, separate symbolic space — the two never appear together. All three remain separate locations, each appearing only where this prompt explicitly places it.
**好在哪**：法头 Geometry lock 里建空间登记表——现实/记忆/走廊三空间各归各 CUT，互斥条款防模型混搭。
**迁移**：多空间叙事写空间登记表（空间名+CUT 归属+互斥条款）。

## 7. 断件追责

### 断环像素级归位 [案例 7L #300]

> The single broken ring…must be **pixel-accurate** to the neck ring shown in <<<c6f61b9a>>> — **same diameter, same link thickness, same weld point, same forged-iron finish** — simply relocated to the ground and shown split open rather than closed.
**好在哪**：断件要求与原物四同（直径/链环厚/焊点/铁艺）+重定位+开裂数。
**迁移**：碎裂物写「pixel-accurate to the original — same [四同] — relocated and shown [状态]」。

### 一体物锁定板 [案例 7L #84]

> <<<da4c4abc>>> locks the tail's ring, chain and ground stake as one single connected object — whenever this whole object is affected, all three parts are treated and rendered as that one unified piece.
**好在哪**：三件散件合成一个资产板——「一体物」声明防模型把它们当独立元素分别变形。
**迁移**：物理联动组件合制一块一体板；prompt 写 whenever this whole object is affected, all parts as one unified piece。

## 2026-11-07 增补：博客池 B2 批次（E401/E405/E407 · 参考制度）

### 参考图数量论 [案例 frank_houbre Lost Garden 导演]
> **the reference carries the look, the prompt carries the motion** ｜ **金句：Count the things that must stay identical rather than the images you happen to have. A folder of twenty pictures of your character is a folder.**
**好在哪**：cousin 病=参考打架→**It splits the difference**（平均拆分）；两三张/角色+不变要素各一；平台上限谱（Runway 硬顶 3——**not a recommendation**）；远景不挂 turnaround（location carries the frame）；**boring on purpose**（Even light, neutral expression, plain background）。
**迁移**：按不变要素数参考；参考图故意无聊；远景镜头省下 turnaround。

### 剧本拆解六类 [案例 frank_houbre]
> **It gets grouped by reference**（按参考分组）；expensive = fails and gets regenerated；**Wardrobe states 三态=三参考**（clean, torn, soaked）；**Continuity carriers**（time of day/weather/injuries——**break quietly**）。
**好在哪**：拆解目的=决定**what has to look identical on page 3 and on page 40**；连续性载体悄悄坏所以要单列。
**迁移**：开拍前列六类清单（角色/服装态/复现场景/特写道具/连续性载体/每镜存活两次之物）。

### 保护指令双向律 [案例 iamsource/brunobenetti 互证]
> iamsource：**The more instructions we added to protect the shot, the more opportunities we created for the model to reinterpret it** ｜ bruno（?）：**Stacking negatives would only have given the model ideas** ｜ 对照 artemysia：repeating an important constraint **reinforces it**
**好在哪**：堆保护/堆负向=给模型重释灵感；但重要约束**重复**是强化。边界=重复同一条约束合法，堆不同禁令危险。
**迁移**：关键锁可重复；禁令清单收敛到高概率失败（同官方 POSITIVE LOCKS only high-probability 原则）。

### the handoff 意图传递链 [案例 iamsource JACOB & LUCAS]
> **What no one talks about enough is the handoff**——idea→shot design→blocking→assets→generation **without being diluted or contradicted** ｜ **金句：A beautiful result is not the same as a controlled result** ｜ **change one thing per run** ｜ 新流程工具：**shot ledgers, authority hierarchies, protected-success lists**
**好在哪**：失败要问**which part of the pipeline owned the failure**；每轮只改一物（五物同改=不知何药起效）。
**迁移**：建 shot 台账+权限层级+受保护成功清单；愿景要 **survive contact with the tools**。

### 文字导/参考锁论金句 [案例 官博 ai-fashion-photo-generator · E714]
> **use text to direct the image, and use references to lock what should not drift**
**好在哪**：文字与参考的分工金句——导 vs 锁一语定界。**四锚定法**：Moodboard（无脸论：**showing the model what world — not describing a vibe**）+Soul HEX 色板+Soul ID 人脸+preset 保持，**跳过四锚→flat lighting and that overprocessed AI sheen**（AI 光泽=锚缺失来源论）。
**迁移**：每项开工作先问"四锚齐了吗"；AI 感过重先查锚缺。

### 角色漂移=记忆问题金句 [案例 官博 why-does-your-ai-characters-face-keep-changing · 22K 大件]

**好在哪**：漂移根因定性（无记忆累积漂移）+修复定性（锚定非措辞）。
**迁移**：漂移时别改 prompt 措辞，去补锚（SoulID/Elements/参考表）。

### @ 引用文件论+类型前缀系统 [案例 官博 ad-multiplier · E777；ai-car-commercial · 26 Element]
